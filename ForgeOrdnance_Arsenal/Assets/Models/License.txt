Third-party models and effect textures in forge-ordnance-arsenal.bundle. Each was downloaded free under the
licence shown on its page, then re-oriented, scaled and re-exported for the game (flat colours of the low-poly models merged
into one small palette texture; textured models keep their own textures, resized, with the ambient occlusion multiplied into the
base colour; effect textures converted to white-on-alpha and packed into atlases). The originals are not included.

Models, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):

  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/m249-machine-gun-game-ready-download-631d0a61696a4703914d6b598b47505a, CC BY 4.0.
  Modified: the telescopic sight and its mount are removed, the carry handle, charging handle and front sight post are removed, the ammo pouch is a separate part, the mesh is reduced to
