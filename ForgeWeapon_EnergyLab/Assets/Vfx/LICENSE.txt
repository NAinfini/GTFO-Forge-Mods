Forge Weapon Energy Lab experimental effect assets
=================================================

forge-energy-disc.bundle
------------------------
Copyright (c) 2026 NAinfini. Licensed under MIT; see ../../LICENSE.
The projectile disc is a native Unity prefab baked from the mod's rotor
geometry, four materials and trail settings.

forge-vfx.bundle
----------------
CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Forge's shared effect bundle, built with Unity 2019.4.21f1. It holds only
Forge's own effect prefabs, particle shaders and self-made textures,
materials and meshes; no third-party file is inside. forge-vfx.manifest.json
lists every asset with its SHA-256, source, author, license and modifications.
