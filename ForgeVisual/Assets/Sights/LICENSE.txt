ForgeVisual sights (red-dot-1x, holo-1x, scope-2x)

Self-made for GTFO Forge on 2026-11-06 in Blender 5.2: hard-surface modelling from primitives by a script (bevelled parts: rail clamp
and bolts, risers, turrets and knurled caps, window frames, hoods, eyecup), split materials (anodised metal, steel, polymer,
rubber, lens coating) and textures baked in Blender from those materials (ambient occlusion and procedural wear and roughness
noise; no photo, downloaded or generated asset, no reference mesh). Part of GTFO Forge and released under the same terms as
ForgeVisual. The shapes follow common real-world sight classes (micro tube red dot, holographic hood, compact prism scope) and copy no
maker's design, name or markings.

Files: <id>.model.json (Forge model package descriptor, in Git); <id>.forgemesh and <id>.t<index>.png (mesh and textures, local media,
not in Git), made by Tools/Models/glb_to_forge_model.py from the .glb the build script exports (source build_sights3.py and the
.blend files are local media).
