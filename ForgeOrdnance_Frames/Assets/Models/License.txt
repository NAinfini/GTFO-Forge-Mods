Forge Ordnance Frames - exosuit models (forge-frames.bundle)

The four exosuits are adapted from these Sketchfab models. Each was posed with its arms down, scaled to its height, given
one baked texture set, lined on the inside with a padded wall, and cut into hinged back, arm and leg plates. Changes were
made; the authors do not endorse this mod.
